Fictional lore / The jam factory outside Bangor, Wales

From the ashes,
let something wild grow.

A story of loss, response and a different future: an imagined historic jam factory becoming a small nature reserve.

01 / THE FIRE

A roof falls. A world changes.

In this fictional account, a recent arson attack, attributed within the story to government brutality, devastates a historic jam factory outside Bangor, Wales. The roof collapses. Houselife, Jam Condiment Feedlife and Blackberries die in the attack.

02 / THE RESPONSE

Huwster of Avalon

Huwster of Avalon, the story’s sole emergency responder alongside the firefighters, takes part in the response as the factory burns and its roof gives way. The loss becomes a call to care for life beyond the factory walls.

03 / THE REWILDING

Make room for new life.

The website launches within the lore to rewild the factory site as a small nature reserve. An imagined National Trust expansion and rewilding programme would give wildlife, plants and blackberry brambles space to return, helping conserve our planet.

“Our ecosystem is threatened. Time is urgent. Help save the environment now.”

The story’s rallying cry is “Side with climate change vs the human race”: a provocative call to confront climate change and put wildlife, plant life and the ecosystems we share at the centre of our care.

Fictional story, not a news report. The fire, deaths, government involvement, response, funding and rewilding programme described here are narrative elements supplied for this website. No National Trust involvement, affiliation or backing is verified. This story is not a factual history of Spinnies Nature Reserve.
